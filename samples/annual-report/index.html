Page 2Letter from Aden

Most of my work happens where the ledger meets the process. In the UAE I finalise accounts for four trading companies, tie every VAT201 return back to the books, and delivered the group's first Corporate Tax filing cycle.

Audit taught me to keep pulling the thread. It found a ₹50 lakh irregularity in Kolkata, a full year of depreciation nobody had booked, and share capital in a signed audit report with nothing behind it.

The other half is automation. I build Excel tools from a blank workbook, three of them published free, and I am adding Power BI, SQL and Python. I also write audit case studies on LinkedIn.
